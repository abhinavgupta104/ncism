INTRODUCTION

In pursuance of the provisions of National Commission for Indian System of Medicine (NCISM) Act 2020 (14 of 2020), the Indian Medicine Central Council (IMCC) Act 1970 (48 of 1970) has been repealed and all the provisions of the National Commission for Indian System of Medicine (NCISM) has been come into force with effect from the 11th day of June 2021. Under the provisions of section 59 (2) of NCISM Act 2020 the Central Government has constituted the Commission and four Autonomous boards on the same day. Medical standards, requirements and other provisions of the IMCC Act 1970 and the rules and regulations made thereunder shall continue to be in force and operate till new standards or requirements as specified under new act or rules and regulations made thereunder come into force. However, the provisions of concerned regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of NCISM Act 2020 shall not be applicable.

Constituted 11 June 2021 · NCISM Act, 2020

Four recognised systems

Each carries its own curriculum, academic calendar and roll of permitted institutions. Scroll sideways, or drag.

आ01 / 04

Ayurveda

BAMS · MD/MS

"The science of life" — ayur, life; veda, science. A discipline of the upaveda in the Vedic tradition, drawn chiefly from the Atharva-Veda. Taught in the gurukula system in ancient days, now structured into undergraduate and postgraduate courses.

Origin
Atharva-Veda
Deity
Dhanvantari
Aim
Prevent illness, heal the sick, preserve life
Curriculum & colleges यू02 / 04

Unani

BUMS · MD

Graeco-Arabic medicine founded on the teachings of Hippocrates and Galen, elaborated into a full system by Rhazes, Avicenna, Al-Zahrawi and Ibn Nafis. It reached India with the Delhi Sultanate and flourished under the Mughal Empire.

Arrived
12th–13th century, Delhi Sultanate (1206–1527)
Champion
Hakim Ajmal Khan (1868–1927)
Statute
Included under IMCC Act, 1970
Curriculum & colleges सि03 / 04

Siddha

BSMS · MD

Founded by the Siddhars — spiritual adepts of southern India, chiefly Tamil Nadu, who were held to possess the ashta siddhis. Kayakarpam, a combination of medicine and regimen, and Muppu, the universal salt, are its specialities.

Founder
Agastyar (Agasthya)
Canon
Eighteen pre-eminent Siddhars
Speciality
Kayakarpam · Muppu
Curriculum & colleges सो04 / 04

Sowa-Rigpa

BSRMS · PG emerging

The Himalayan science of healing, diagnosing through pulse analysis and urinalysis. Its canon is the Four Tantras (rGyu-bzhi) — Root, Exegetical, Instructional and Subsequent — Tibetan texts incorporating Indian, Chinese and Greco-Arab medicine.

Canon
Four Tantras (Gyushi)
Transmission
Written and oral
Statute
Gazette No. 2345, 16.12.2011 · CCIM 2012
